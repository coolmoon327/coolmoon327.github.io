## Before Writing (2–3 Weeks)

1. Research schools, find the  personal statement  requirements and application materials on their official websites, and familiarize yourself with the process.
2. Carefully organize and document all application-relevant experiences from university: grades, internships, campus activities, teaching, and research.
3. Select the most relevant information for the application materials.
4. A useful writing order is résumé, personal statement, recommendation letters (provided by the recommenders), and prompted essays.

## During Writing

1. Motivation for choosing the major
2. Main experiences: relevant educational background, research project experiences, internship experiences
3. Areas of interest and motivation for choosing the specific school
4. Career planning

- The first is suitable for PhD applications.
  - There is little difference between applying for a PhD and a research-based master's program.
- The second section is organized by acquired skills, where you can summarize your undergraduate experiences in this area.
- You can demonstrate your problem-solving ability and resilience here—for example, describe a problem you resolved through sustained effort—and present it using `becec`.

### Paragraph 4: Internship Experience

- While economics and management programs emphasize internships, we place greater emphasis on scientific research.
  - During an internship at China Telecom, I learned about an existing problem, contributed work involving MADDPG, and subsequently published a paper.

![image-20221128173358015](/assets/blog/generated/nas-import/post-47a760c28ea1/image-011.png)

### Paragraph 5: Why This School

- Avoid mass-applying with a single template; your content must be tailored and not generic.
- Always review the program website: for graduate studies, focus on courses; for doctoral programs, focus on professors and research directions.
  - I am interested in xxx, and your university offers relevant courses that I hope to take to enhance my skills.
  - There is a problem I have not yet resolved that I aim to address during my PhD; Professor xxx  would be an ideal mentor for this.
- The school's xxx philosophy aligns closely with my own.

## Post-Writing: Refinement and Revision

1. Personal statements are also subject to plagiarism checks.
2. Adjust the structure and content according to different schools and programs.
3. Have teachers, classmates, or friends who are proficient in English help with polishing.
