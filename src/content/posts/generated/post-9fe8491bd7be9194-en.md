# LaTeX

## Common Text-Formatting Styles

- **Bold**:
  - ‘\textbf{文本}’
- *Italic*:
  - ‘\textit{意大利斜体}’
  - ‘\textsl{Slanted 斜体}’
- ABCDA\_{BC}DABC​D:
  - ‘\textsc{AbcD}’
- A few commands whose purpose I am unsure about:
  - ‘\textnormal{默认}’
  - ‘\textmd{中等权重}’
  - ‘\textup{显示直立文本}’
