## Perspective

The field-level value lies in moving pinching-antenna multiple access beyond deterministic line-of-sight assumptions and into explicit outage-aware design under blockage and scattering.

## Outage-Constrained Environment Division Multiple Access (EDMA) for Pinching-Antenna Systems

**Authors:** Weihao Mao, Yang Lu, Yanqing Xu, Zhiguo Ding

**Status:** Preprint

[Open the primary public record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18738). From a resilient-access perspective, the preprint turns blockage, scattering, and in-waveguide attenuation into explicit statistical rate-outage constraints. Exact two-user expressions establish the baseline before PGD and a Chernoff/SCA multi-user approximation are introduced.

**Evidence boundary.** The result is a numerical access-design study: near-optimality is scenario-bound, and the scalable multi-user formulation trades exactness for tractability.
