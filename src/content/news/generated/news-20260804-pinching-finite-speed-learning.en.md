## Perspective

The field-level contribution is a co-design problem: antenna actuation, device selection, training, and freshness are optimized together instead of treating movement as instantaneous.

## Age-of-Information Aware Federated Learning with Finite Speed Pinching Antenna

**Authors:** Kaidi Wang, Daniel K C So, Zhiguo Ding

**Status:** Preprint

[Open the primary public record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23595). For learning-enabled wireless design, the important change is to make antenna motion part of each federated-learning round. Device participation, placement, local training, upload time, and information age are therefore coupled rather than optimized in isolation.

**Evidence boundary.** The coalition and branch-and-bound solution is evaluated in simulation; its optimality statement belongs to the defined placement region, not to end-to-end learning or measured actuation energy.
