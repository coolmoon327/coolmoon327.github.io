## Outage-Constrained Environment Division Multiple Access (EDMA) for Pinching-Antenna Systems

**Authors:** Weihao Mao, Yang Lu, Yanqing Xu, Zhiguo Ding

**Status:** Preprint

[Open the primary public record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18738). In Zhiguo Ding’s recent pinching-antenna work, EDMA is extended to uncertain propagation through outage-constrained deployment and power allocation. The analysis moves from an exact two-user expression to PGD and a Chernoff/SCA multi-user construction.

**Evidence boundary.** The research progression is analytical rather than experimental: the reported near-optimality is simulation-specific, and the multi-user result relies on approximation.
